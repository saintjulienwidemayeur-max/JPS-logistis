Holidays marked on the calendar:
- **Orange circle**: Haiti public holiday
- **Cyan ring**: U.S. federal holiday

Moveable dates (Carnival, Good Friday, Ascension, Corpus Christi, MLK Day, Thanksgiving, etc.) are
computed from Easter 2027 (March 28) in `web/caldata.js`.
